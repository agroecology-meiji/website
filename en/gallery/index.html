2026

Activity log

Sep. 14

We went to Chichibu for a lab retreat. Lab members proposed and planned the seminar program from scratch, leading to many new ideas.

Sep. 10

We collected ants at Kurokawa Farm. Because ant species differ in their ecology, we combined multiple methods to collect comprehensive data.

Sep. 9

We visited RIKEN in Tsukuba for instrument measurements and experienced the distinctive atmosphere of a research institute.

Sep. 4

We visited a farm practicing no-till cultivation and experienced the vitality and diversity of living organisms.

Aug. 20

We conducted a survey in a dwarf pine forest on Mount Norikura. Koizumi has continued research at this field site since graduate school.

Aug. 2

At the Open Campus event, each laboratory in the School of Agriculture set up a booth. Our first cohort introduced their upcoming research and answered questions from prospective students.

July 20

We harvested vegetables grown in our campus field. A colorful variety of crops had developed, and we look forward to seeing what the coming season brings.

July 12

We presented our research at the International Conference on Mycorrhiza 2026. The results on seasonal change and phenology were presented in the session titled "Seasons Change."

July 2

Many mushrooms had appeared at Ikuta Shrine on the Ikuta Campus, so we held an impromptu mushroom walk before our seminar.

June 17

We set up pitfall traps to collect ants as a pilot study ahead of full-scale data collection. We later added rain covers and left the traps in place for three days.

May 18

We visited Kurokawa Farm to discuss plans for undergraduate research and learned about crop management in each field section.

May 11

We planted seedlings in the field after discussing what and how to plant with all third-year students.

Apr. 24

We started a lab field on the Ikuta Campus. The first step was to prepare the field.
